CLASSROOM 01 — GENESIS

It All Started in This Classroom…

This idea literally came from our own classroom — Sara and Darsana’s classroom. 😂

We noticed something happening right in front of us.

Some students had mastered the art of hiding from the teacher — sitting in those “safe” seats, ducking behind other students, and making sure they were just out of the teacher’s line of sight.

And we were like…

“Wait… why are some seats safer than others?” 👀

That random observation turned into a question:

“What if we could actually measure how visible a student is from the teacher’s position?”

And that’s where it all started.

No big research.
No complicated problem statement.

Just students hiding from the teacher in our classroom…

…and two of us thinking:

“Okay, this could actually be a project.” 💀
THE ORIGINAL IDEA
CLASSROOM 02 — SCREEN 01

From a Classroom Observation to USELESS

Inspired by our classroom chaos, we decided to turn the teacher’s point of view into a camera. 📹

This screen shows exactly what the teacher would see from the front of the classroom — except the teacher has been replaced by a camera. 😭

The camera detects every student and gives them a Visibility Score out of 100.

Higher Score → More Visible → Easier for the teacher to spot 👀

Lower Score → Less Visible → Better chances of staying unnoticed 😎

So basically, the camera is doing what the teacher normally does:

Look around → Detect students → Judge their visibility → Give them a score. 💀

No teacher was required for this calculation.

THE CAMERA HAS OFFICIALLY
TAKEN OVER THE TEACHER’S JOB. 😂
